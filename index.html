AURA Health

AI Healthcare Command Center

Designing a smarter clinical workspace that helps healthcare teams understand patients, coordinate care, and act faster with AI-powered insights — bringing fragmented workflows into one focused environment.

Overview

A calmer interface for complex clinical work.

AURA Health is an AI-powered healthcare command center designed to bring patient information, clinical workflows, and intelligent insights into one focused workspace. The goal was simple yet ambitious: reduce the noise, surface what matters, and help clinicians make faster, better-informed decisions.

Instead of jumping between six disconnected systems, clinicians get one intelligent workspace that understands context, prioritizes action, and keeps the human at the center of every decision.

The Challenge

Healthcare data is powerful, but fragmented.

Healthcare professionals often work across disconnected systems for patient records, appointments, laboratory results, medications, and treatment plans. This fragmentation creates friction, cognitive overload, and slows down care.

User Research

Understanding the clinical workflow.

Interviews with clinicians revealed that the biggest friction isn't lack of data — it's fragmented presentation. Every extra click, every disconnected system, every manual scan costs time that should be spent on patients.
