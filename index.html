Overview

AURA Health is an AI‑powered healthcare command center designed to bring patient information, clinical workflows and intelligent insights into one focused workspace.

The Challenge

Healthcare data is powerful, but fragmented.

Healthcare professionals often work across disconnected systems for patient records, appointments, laboratory results, medications and treatment plans.

Information overload
Fragmented workflows
Difficult patient history navigation
Design Goal

One intelligent workspace for every clinical decision.

01

Reduce Cognitive Load

Make critical patient information easier to understand at a glance.

02

Accelerate Workflows

Reduce unnecessary navigation and repetitive work.

03

Assist Better Decisions

Use AI to surface relevant insights while keeping clinicians in control.

User Research
