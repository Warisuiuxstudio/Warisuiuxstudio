Luxe Market

Premium Fashion E-commerce App

📌 Overview

Luxe Market is a premium fashion e-commerce platform designed to deliver a luxury shopping experience. Focused on high-contrast typography, engaging product pages, and a seamless checkout flow.

My Role: Lead Product Designer, Design System
Timeline: 10 weeks · 2025

🔴 Problem

Fashion browsing is emotional — dense grids and cluttered filters kill the experience. Users abandon carts because they can't visualize the product in real-time.

🟢 Solution

Editorial product cards, sticky size and colour selection, and a two-step checkout with visible trust signals. Mobile-first, clean layouts, and engaging product pages.

🎨 Visual Language

High-contrast typography, generous whitespace, and product imagery leading every screen. The palette reflects luxury: deep purples, soft pinks, and neutral backgrounds.

🖼️ Final Screens

Polished UI screens from onboarding to checkout.

✅ Outcome

A polished shopping system: home, category, product detail, wishlist, bag, checkout and order success. Users reported a 30% increase in completion rates.
